A, Principal-component analysis based on the 1,000 most variable analysed genes. Axis percentages refer to variance in this PCA input. No samples were excluded on the basis of PCA. B, Pearson sample-correlation matrix with samples ordered by frozen metadata for display. C, Differential-expression overview showing all tested genes. Coloured points passed BH q<0.05 and absolute log2 fold change >0.5. D, Row-scaled expression of the 40 genes with the largest absolute moderated t statistics in the same contrast. Values were standardised within each gene and truncated at ±2.5. Because these genes were selected from the displayed contrast, the heatmap is descriptive and is not independent validation or classification. S4a shows GSE153434, comprising ascending-aorta tissue from 10 controls and 10 people with aortic dissection. S4b shows GSE38750, comprising iliac arteries from 15 people with uraemia and renal arteries from 19 controls. Disease status and arterial sampling site are therefore completely confounded in GSE38750; its panels cannot separate uraemia from arterial-bed biology or establish that different arteries are interchangeable. These quality-control and descriptive displays do not establish temporal order or renal-to-aortic causality.
