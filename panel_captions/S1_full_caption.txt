These plots are descriptive quality-control summaries and were not used to exclude samples after viewing group separation. C,D, Pairwise Spearman correlation matrices for all samples retained in the two primary animal comparisons. Samples were ordered by the frozen primary group, then reported sex and sample identifier for display only; facet strips mark the primary groups. Compact labels are used in the figure, while full expression-column identifiers and GEO accessions remain in Source Data. No sample was excluded using the displayed ordering or observed correlations. Colour limits are stated independently for each dataset and should not be used to compare absolute correlation structure across platforms. E, Voom mean–variance diagnostic for the GSE325608 primary model. Points show gene-wise mean log2 expression and square-root residual standard deviation; the fitted trend was used to calculate observation-level precision weights. GSE146638 contained processed continuous expression and was analysed using limma with trend moderation rather than voom. These panels assess data structure and model diagnostics; they do not independently validate differential expression or cross-model molecular convergence.
