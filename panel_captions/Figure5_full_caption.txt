The schematic summarises the prespecified interpretation of the study's evidence layers; it introduces no new data or statistical analysis. Shared transcriptional programmes in uraemic vascular calcification and source-defined dissection-or-aneurysm animal models were followed by narrower, context-dependent human signals. Ossification passed correction in all three human datasets, extracellular matrix organization passed in two, and smooth-muscle findings varied by dataset. Genetic analyses did not establish a renal-to-dissection relationship: no primary Mendelian-randomisation estimate passed BH correction, LD-score genetic correlation was non-estimable, colocalisation was prior-sensitive and reverse Mendelian randomisation was instrument-limited. Immune programmes did not pass correction in either human dataset, and classifier checks did not establish renal-origin specificity or consistent external discrimination. The retained claim is therefore a bounded arterial-remodelling tissue-state overlap, with human support strongest for ossification-related expression. Rows distinguish evidence layers, with findings in the middle column and interpretation boundaries on the right. Row dimensions and accent colours are non-quantitative and do not encode effect size, sample size or evidential weight. The figure does not imply causality, mediation, therapeutic action or prediction of future aortic-dissection risk.
