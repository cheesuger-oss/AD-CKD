AAD denotes the source-defined aortic dissection or aneurysm group. A, GO Biological Process and KEGG over-representation results. Within each animal model and database, the five significant terms with the smallest BH-adjusted P values are displayed. Position denotes fold enrichment, point size denotes the number of mapped differential-expression genes and colour denotes −log10(BH q). B, GO Biological Process detail showing the ten significant terms with the smallest BH-adjusted P values in each animal model. Terms were selected by adjusted P value; bar length represents fold enrichment and begins at zero. C–F, Deterministically selected all-ranked GSEA summaries for GO Biological Process and KEGG in each animal model. One term per model and database was selected by the smallest BH-adjusted P value, with absolute normalised enrichment score used only to break ties. Bars show the normalised enrichment score; the selected term, identifier, BH-adjusted P value and gene-set size are reported in each panel. Displayed terms were selected after completion of the animal enrichment analyses and were not prespecified. Complete frozen ORA and GSEA tables are linked in Source Data and Supplementary Table S2. ORA is directionless, whereas GSEA signs refer to the case-minus-control gene ranking; the two layers need not select the same terms and are not independent replication. KEGG disease and infection labels identify annotated gene sets and do not establish infection or another diagnosis in the samples. These enrichment results describe transcriptional programmes and do not establish cell composition, tissue histology, a causal mechanism or a renal-to-dissection causal pathway.
