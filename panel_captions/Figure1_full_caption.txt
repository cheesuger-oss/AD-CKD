A, Frozen animal comparisons. AAD denotes the source-defined aortic dissection or aneurysm group; NonAD denotes the source-defined exposed comparison group. The uraemic vascular calcification analysis compared five uraemic with five untreated rat aortas. The primary experimental AAD analysis compared 16 β-aminopropionitrile (BAPN)-exposed mice assigned to the AAD group with 20 untreated controls and included sex in the differential-expression model. Twelve BAPN-exposed NonAD mice were excluded from the primary contrast and retained for the exposure-matched sensitivity analysis. B, Direction of the 549 differential-expression genes shared after mapping each animal result to human one-to-one orthologues. When multiple source symbols mapped to the same frozen human orthologue, the estimate with the largest absolute log2 fold change was retained. Of the 549 genes, 327 increased and 151 decreased in both models; 71 changed in opposite directions. The exposure-matched AAD-versus-BAPN-nonAD sensitivity model was also adjusted for sex and identified 1,963 genes at the same BH q<0.05 and absolute log2-fold-change>0.5 threshold. This comparison reduces, but does not remove, differences unrelated to the AAD lesion phenotype. C,D, Volcano plots for all 9,814 genes tested in the uraemic vascular calcification comparison and all 14,326 genes tested in the primary experimental AAD comparison. Coloured points passed BH q<0.05 and absolute log2-fold-change>0.5; grey points did not. The vertical and horizontal dashed lines mark these thresholds. The 549-gene intersection is descriptive and contains both concordant and discordant changes; pathway-level analyses provide the main evidence for cross-model molecular overlap. The rat case group underwent nephrectomy, high-phosphate feeding and alfacalcidol treatment; the contrast does not isolate renal dysfunction from these co-treatments.
