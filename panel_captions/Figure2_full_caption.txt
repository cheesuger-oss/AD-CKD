A, Six vascular-remodelling focus programmes selected from the original 155 GO/KEGG terms that passed BH q<0.01 in both animal-model differential-expression over-representation analyses; these programmes were subsequently frozen for human evaluation. Correction of date-converted rat identifiers reduced the shared intersection to 154 terms, with all six frozen programmes retained without reselection. The uraemic vascular calcification comparison included five uraemic and five untreated animals. The experimental AAD comparison included 16 BAPN-exposed animals with source-defined dissection or aneurysm (AAD) and 20 untreated controls, with sex included in the differential-expression model. Points show −log10(BH-adjusted P); the dashed line marks q=0.01. Only the six focus programmes, rather than all 154 shared terms, are displayed. This thresholded analysis is directionless and does not imply that constituent genes changed in the same direction. B, All-ranked GSEA sensitivity analysis of the same six focus programmes in both animal models. Points show normalised enrichment scores; filled points passed BH correction within the corresponding analysis. This is a separate evidence layer and need not mirror DEG over-representation. C, The same six programmes tested separately in human aortic-dissection tissue (GSE153434), cross-site uraemic vascular tissue (GSE38750) and same-site internal-mammary-artery tissue (GSE38751). Filled points passed BH correction across exactly six programmes within the corresponding dataset. Negative NES values indicate enrichment toward the control end of each case-minus-control ranking and do not indicate absence of pathway biology. Ossification passed correction in all three datasets. Extracellular-matrix organization passed correction in GSE153434 and GSE38750 and had the same direction but q=0.0920 in GSE38751. Smooth-muscle contraction passed correction in GSE153434 and GSE38751. Two hierarchically nested proliferation terms passed correction only in GSE38751 and are not independent biological replications. D, Human sampling design. GSE153434 compared ascending-aorta tissue from 10 aortic-dissection cases and 10 controls. GSE38750 compared iliac arteries from 15 uraemic cases with renal arteries from 19 controls; disease status and arterial site were completely confounded. GSE38751 compared internal mammary arteries from five cases and five controls, reducing arterial-site confounding but remaining a small companion series from the same study and publication as GSE38750. These human results show recurrent, context-dependent programme-level signals, not proof of an identical transcriptional state, histological remodelling or a causal renal-to-aortic pathway. The rat case group underwent nephrectomy, high-phosphate feeding and alfacalcidol treatment; the contrast does not isolate renal dysfunction from these co-treatments.
