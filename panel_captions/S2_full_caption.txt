AAD denotes the source-defined aortic dissection or aneurysm group. A,B, Row-scaled expression of the 30 differential-expression genes with the largest absolute moderated t statistics in the uraemic vascular calcification and primary experimental AAD comparisons. Values were standardised within each gene and truncated at ±2.5 for display. Samples are grouped by the frozen primary comparison. These genes were selected using the same contrasts shown in the heatmaps; the panels are descriptive and are not independent validation or classification analyses. C, Volcano plot for the sex-adjusted comparison of 16 β-aminopropionitrile (BAPN)-exposed mice assigned to the AAD group with 12 BAPN-exposed mice assigned to the NonAD group. All tested genes are shown. The analysis identified 1,963 differentially expressed genes at BH q<0.05 and absolute log2-fold-change>0.5; grey points did not pass both thresholds. D, Comparison of primary AAD-versus-untreated and exposure-matched AAD-versus-BAPN-nonAD log2 fold changes for genes tested in both models. The two contrasts shared the same 16 AAD samples. Colour identifies genes meeting the sensitivity-analysis differential-expression threshold; the reported Spearman coefficient summarises rank association rather than agreement. The exposure-matched analysis reduces confounding by BAPN exposure but does not isolate a causal AAD effect, and similarity between effect estimates does not constitute independent replication.
