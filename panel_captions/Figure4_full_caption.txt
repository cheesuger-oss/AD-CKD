A, Standardised case-control differences for six prespecified immune-expression programmes in the uraemic vascular calcification dataset (five cases and five controls), human aortic-dissection dataset (ten cases and ten controls) and human uraemic vascular dataset (15 cases and 19 controls). Bars show approximate Wald 95% confidence intervals for the uncorrected standardised mean differences. Filled points passed limma tests with BH correction across the six programmes within the corresponding dataset; open points did not. The confidence intervals and limma/BH symbols are separate summaries and need not yield identical threshold decisions. These scores describe expression programmes and do not estimate immune-cell proportions. B, Distribution of out-of-fold ROC AUC values from 100 size-matched random-gene sets evaluated with the same modelling pipeline as the renal-derived feature set in 20 samples (ten aortic-dissection cases and ten controls). Expression preprocessing used the full cohort; feature restriction, scaling and tuning were fold-internal. Internal performance may therefore be optimistic. The observed renal-derived model AUC was 1.00; 94 of 100 random-gene controls also achieved AUC=1.00 and the 95th percentile was 1.00, so the internal result did not support renal-origin specificity. C, Fixed coefficient-weighted percentile-rank signature ROC AUCs and stratified-bootstrap 95% confidence intervals (2,000 resamples) in the prespecified external cohorts, with no cohort-specific refitting: GSE52093, seven cases and five controls; GSE98770, six cases and five controls; GSE147026, four cases and four controls; and GSE190635, four cases and four controls. GSE147026 remained in the frozen audit order but was not evaluable because the available series matrix contained no expression measurements. These retrospective analyses concern case-control tissue discrimination and do not evaluate prediction of future aortic-dissection risk.
