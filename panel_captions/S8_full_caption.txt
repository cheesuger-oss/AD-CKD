A, Sample-level scores for the six frozen immune-expression programmes in GSE146638, GSE153434 and GSE38750, standardised within each dataset and programme for display. These values describe coordinated expression programmes and do not estimate immune-cell proportions; z scores are not comparable as absolute levels across datasets. B,C, Receiver operating characteristic and precision–recall curves from the 20 out-of-fold predictions in the primary GSE153434 tissue-classification analysis. Expression preprocessing used the full cohort; only feature restriction, scaling and tuning were nested within training folds. These diagnostics may therefore retain optimism. The internal ROC AUC and average precision were both 1.00, but size-matched random-gene controls performed similarly, so these panels do not establish renal-origin specificity. D, Exploratory calibration summary of the same out-of-fold predictions in five probability groups. This small-sample retrospective diagnostic does not establish population calibration or prediction of future aortic-dissection risk. E, Coefficients of the locked 27-gene elastic-net tissue signature. F, Thirty genes with the highest selection frequencies across 500 class-stratified bootstrap fits. Coefficients and selection frequencies are associative model diagnostics, not causal effects, biological perturbations or therapeutic evidence.
